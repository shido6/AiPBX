# Howtos

Short, practical guides for pushing AiPBX further. These are **not** the
administrator documentation (that is in [docs/](../docs/README.md), ships with
every installation and is mirrored to the portal and the website): a howto
here describes something optional, built on top of a working PBX, usually with
a third-party service that is billed separately.

| Howto | What it does |
|-------|--------------|
| [Give Greg a face and a voice with Anam.ai](greg-anam-avatar.md) | An optional video avatar in Greg's chat: answers spoken aloud, an optional two-way listening mode, and meeting invites |

## Writing one

- Say what it costs and what it needs before the steps: an account, a key, a
  paid plan, minutes per month.
- No ids, keys, host names or personal accounts in the repository. Every
  installation uses its own, entered in the portal — the howto explains where
  to get them and nothing more.
- Test the steps on a real installation, and say which version you tested.
